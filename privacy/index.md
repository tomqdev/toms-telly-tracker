*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

This policy covers the Tom's Telly Tracker Android app.

## Who I am

Tom's Telly Tracker is made by Tom Queen, an individual developer in the UK. Contact: **tom.queen.dev@gmail.com**.

## The short version

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It sends nothing about you to me.

## What stays on your phone

The app stores these on your phone only:

- the shows you track, their episodes and release dates;
- which episodes you've marked as watched;
- your region, your chosen streaming services and your settings.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy this data to your Google account, and to a new phone when you transfer to one. That backup is run by Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and I can't see it.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it from your phone.

## What the app sends, and to whom

To find shows and keep them up to date, the app sends requests directly from your phone to two data services:

- **[TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/)** receives your show searches and the IDs of the shows you track. Show posters also load from TVmaze. See [TVmaze's privacy policy](https://www.tvmaze.com/site/privacy).
- **[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/)** receives the IDs of the shows you track and your region, to find which services carry them. Service logos also load from TMDB. See [TMDB's privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

Like any website, these services see your phone's IP address when it connects to them. Neither service receives your watched list, and I receive none of these requests.

## Feedback emails

If you choose to email me, from the app's Help & feedback rows or otherwise, I receive your email address and what you write, plus the app version, phone model and region if you leave them in. I use them only to reply to you and to improve the app. I don't share them with anyone, and I'll delete them if you ask.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.

## Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you (at most, your feedback emails) or ask me to delete it, at **tom.queen.dev@gmail.com**. In the UK you can also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
