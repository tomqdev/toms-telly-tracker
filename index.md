title: Tom's Telly Tracker
---

Tom's Telly Tracker is a free Android app that tells you when new episodes of your favourite shows come out where you live, and which of your streaming services has them. It has no ads, no account and no tracking.

- **This week** shows what's out and what's coming this week, with release dates for your region.
- **My shows** keeps the shows you follow, with their next episode and where to watch it.
- **Notifications** can tell you when an episode is out, what's out today, when a new date is announced, and what's coming each week.

## Report a bug or suggest an idea

In the app, go to **Settings → Help & feedback** and tap **Report a bug** or **Suggest an idea**. Your email app opens with the app version, your phone model and your region filled in, so I can reproduce the problem.

Or email **tom.queen.dev@gmail.com** directly.

## Notifications arriving late?

Some phones delay notifications to save battery. In the app, go to **Settings → Notifications arriving late?**, which opens the app's system settings, then set battery use to **Unrestricted**. On Samsung phones this is under **Settings → Apps → Tom's Telly Tracker → Battery**.

## Where the data comes from

- Episode schedules and broadcast dates come from [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/), under the [CC BY-SA licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Streaming services come from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- TMDB's streaming data is provided by [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/).

Tom's Telly Tracker is not affiliated with any broadcaster or streaming service. Show names and service names belong to their owners.
